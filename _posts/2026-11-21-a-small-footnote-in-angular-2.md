Ten years is a long time in software. Links die, services change, and bits of your own history quietly disappear with them.

While going back through some old open-source work, I found a small entry I had forgotten: I was included among the contributors around the Angular 2 release.

It was a tiny contribution, and I was never part of the Angular team. Still, that is one of the nice things about open source: a small piece of work can become part of something much bigger. In 2015, `rixrix` appeared among the top contributors to Angular 2.0.0-alpha.37. A year later, Richard Sentino appeared in the acknowledgement list for the final Angular 2.0 release.

Angular 2.0 was announced on 22 September 2016. The official announcement has the full contributor acknowledgement, the release discussion is preserved on Reddit, and GitClear records the alpha.37 release.

Sources:

- <https://opensource.googleblog.com/2016/09/angular-version-2-proprioception.html>
- <https://www.reddit.com/r/Angular2/comments/52u6j3/angular_200_available/>
- <https://www.gitclear.com/open_repos/angular/angular/release/2.0.0-alpha.37>

It is only a footnote, but it is mine. I am glad I found it again.
