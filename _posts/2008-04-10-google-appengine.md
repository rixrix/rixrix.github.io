I was able to sign up for a free account of [Google App Engine](http://code.google.com/appengine/), which had been released a few days earlier and first appeared in a [TechCrunch headline](http://www.techcrunch.com/2008/04/07/google-jumps-head-first-into-web-services-with-google-app-engine/). I tried hitting the App Engine link from TechCrunch and it was not yet ready, but after a few refreshes the App Engine site went live and was ready to accept beta testers. If I'm not mistaken, I probably belonged to the first ten people who got the free account.

> [**Google App Engine**](http://code.google.com/appengine/) enables you to build web applications on the same scalable systems that power Google applications.

**Update: April 11, 2008**

[Eclipse 3.3](http://www.eclipse.org/home/categories/languages.php) + [PyDev](http://pydev.sourceforge.net/) + [Google App Engine](http://code.google.com/appengine/)

The [original linked setup](http://daily.profeth.de/2008/04/google-app-engine-eclipse-pydev.html) worked fine in my Xubuntu Gutsy.
