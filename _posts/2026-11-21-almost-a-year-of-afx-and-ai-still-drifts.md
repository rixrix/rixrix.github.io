It's been almost a year since I built [AgenticFlowX (AFX)](https://agenticflowx.github.io/). Since then, I've managed to build a few open-source projects, ship some into production, and generate a decent amount of code along the way.

One thing I've learned: no matter how advanced the models get, even Claude Fable 5.1 (High), they still drift from the original specs. Sometimes subtly, sometimes completely off track.

After nearly a year of dealing with this, I'd say I've gained a decent understanding of spec-driven development, including where it falls short.

So, I'm building Qeps, the next incarnation of AFX. The bigger plan is to go from a VS Code extension to an MCP service, keeping specifications and code in sync as development progresses.

Qeps is designed for teams and organisations, connecting everyone from CTOs, architects, product owners and project managers down to developers and multiple AI coding agents. The idea is to keep everyone, including the agents, working from the same specifications.
