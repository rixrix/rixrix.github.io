I’ve been meaning to get back into blogging for a while. There was always something else to finish first. Anyway, here’s a post. That seems like a good enough start.

Lately I’ve been doing a lot of AI-assisted coding on side projects. Some of those projects are tools I’ve built to help with the coding itself. Others are things I wanted to build anyway, and they’ve given me a place to try out new ways of working.

Something about it surprises me almost every day. Sometimes a new tool does more than I expected. Sometimes an experiment sends me down a rabbit hole I hadn’t planned on. I’d like to write those things down while I still remember what surprised me.

So that’s what I’d like to use this blog for. A few paragraphs on what I tried, what I found, or what I’m still figuring out. Some days that’ll be about AI. Other days it’ll be whatever happened while I was writing software.
