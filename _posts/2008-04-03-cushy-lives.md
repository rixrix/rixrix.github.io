From [thefreedictionary](http://www.thefreedictionary.com/cushy):

> **cushy** — *adj.* not burdensome or demanding; borne or done easily and without hardship; “what a cushy job!”.

About a month ago, I blogged about this project and things are starting to take shape. Tools, references, development environment, and other necessary information are just within reach. This is my attempt to work on virtual machines, compilers, parsers, and lexical analyzers — *e.g. antlr, yacc, flex/lex, bison*.

The basic idea of cushy is to port some of the best functionality of a new/agile language to an old (not that really old) dynamic/scripting language. I'll be adding more details about the two languages I mentioned once a working prototype is in place. This is going to be exciting and, at the same time, a real pain — a bumpy ride — but if things work according to plan, I'll say:

> **cushy** — do more with less, agile development.

Anyway, I'm still holding my paper and pen; nothing is shown and nothing is built. Stay tuned.
