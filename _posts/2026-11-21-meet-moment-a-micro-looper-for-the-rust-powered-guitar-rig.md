Oxidal, a Rust-powered guitar rig, now has a new effect called Moment. This micro-looper takes short fragments of your playing and turns them into layered textures. The Caught Moments preset gives you a place to start exploring the sound.

The web interface is easier to use on small screens, with larger touch targets and clearer Wi-Fi and hotspot settings. You can also reconnect to a saved network from Settings.

Next up is more flexible scene switching. Work is underway on scenes that can switch several effects on or off and adjust the gain and blend of individual amp and cabinet captures, all with one tap.
